# Spirit Fox: The Lost Magic

*A magical 2D platformer adventure set in a mysterious forest.*

## About the Game

**Spirit Fox: The Lost Magic** is a 2D platformer game developed with JavaScript and Phaser. The player takes on the role of a spirit fox exploring a magical world, collecting crystals, avoiding dangerous obstacles, and fighting enemies.

The game follows a mysterious forest whose ancient magic has been threatened by a dark presence. Players must navigate challenging levels and overcome enemies to restore the lost magic.

## Game Features

- **Story Mode** – Play through the adventure from the beginning to the final boss.
- **Level Mode** – Play individual levels independently.
- **Collectible Crystals** – Collect green and red crystals to earn points.
- **Platforming Challenges** – Jump between platforms, avoid spikes, and navigate dangerous environments.
- **Enemies and Combat** – Encounter flying enemies and use projectiles to fight back.
- **Boss Battle** – Face the Shadow Eagle in the final confrontation.
- **Game Timer and Scoring** – Track completion times and scores.
- **Lives and Game Over System** – Manage your lives and restart after defeat.
- **Save and Progress Features** – Save supported Story Mode progress and track level results.
- **Atmospheric Audio** – Enjoy background music and sound effects throughout the adventure.

## How to Run

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the `spirit-fox-the-lost-magic` project folder in Visual Studio Code.
3. Launch `index.html` using a local development server, such as the Live Server extension.
4. Open the provided local URL in your browser.

## Game Modes

### Story Mode
Progress through the levels in sequence, collect points, and face the final boss. Completing the adventure leads to an end-of-game summary.

### Level Mode
Choose an individual level and play it independently.
